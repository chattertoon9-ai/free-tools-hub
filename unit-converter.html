Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time and data — including the two most searched conversions on the web: cm to inches and kg to lbs.

How to use

  1. Pick a category (Length is selected first).
  2. Type a value and choose the “from” and “to” units.
  3. The result updates as you type — use Swap to reverse the conversion.

Quick reference

Worked example

A parcel weighs 12 kg. In pounds: 12 ÷ 0.45359237 = 26.46 lbs. A doorway is 180 cm tall: 180 ÷ 2.54 = 70.87 inches (5 ft 10.9 in).

Why does temperature convert differently?

Celsius and Fahrenheit have different zero points, so temperature uses a formula instead of a simple multiplier. All other categories here are pure ratios.

How accurate is this converter?

It uses the exact international definitions and shows up to 6 significant decimals, which is more precision than tape measures, scales or thermometers can measure.

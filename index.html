Worked example

A parcel weighs 12 kg. In pounds: 12 ÷ 0.45359237 = 26.46 lbs. A doorway is 180 cm tall: 180 ÷ 2.54 = 70.87 inches (5 ft 10.9 in).

Why does temperature convert differently?

Celsius and Fahrenheit have different zero points, so temperature uses a formula instead of a simple multiplier. All other categories here are pure ratios.

How accurate is this converter?

It uses the exact international definitions and shows up to 6 significant decimals, which is more precision than tape measures, scales or thermometers can measure.

BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index from your height and weight, with the WHO category and the healthy weight range for your height.

Tip: 5 ft 7 in = 170 cm · 154 lbs = 69.9 kg. Use the Unit Converter if you only know imperial units.

How BMI is calculated

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For 70 kg at 1.70 m: 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2, inside the healthy range.

What BMI does not tell you

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, and it is not designed for children, pregnant people or elite athletes. Treat it as a screening number, not a diagnosis — talk to a doctor for health advice.

What is a healthy BMI for adults?

The WHO healthy range for adults is 18.5 to 24.9. This calculator also shows the matching healthy weight range in kg for your exact height.

Is BMI different for men and women?

The formula and adult categories are the same. Body composition differs on average, which is one reason BMI is a guide rather than a verdict.

Age Calculator

Your exact age in years, months and days — plus total days lived, the weekday you were born, and when your next birthday falls.

Why an exact age matters

Forms for exams, jobs, passports and school admissions often ask for age “as on” a specific date — for example, age on 31 December of the admission year. Set the second date to that cut-off to get the exact figure the form expects, including whether a birthday has passed yet.

How it works

The calculator subtracts the dates properly across month lengths and leap years, then breaks the span into years, months and days, and also totals it in days, weeks and hours.

How many days have I lived?

Enter your birth date and leave the second date as today — the “Total days” figure in the result is your exact count, leap days included.

What weekday was I born on?

It is shown in the result. The weekday is computed from the calendar date itself, so it is correct for any date the picker allows.

Percentage Calculator

Three everyday percentage jobs in one place: find a percentage of a number, find what percentage one number is of another, and measure a percentage increase or decrease.

1 · What is X% of Y?

2 · X is what % of Y?

3 · Percentage change

Everyday examples

  • Exam marks: 450 out of 600 = 75% (mode 2).
  • Discount: 15% off Rs 2,400 = Rs 360 off, so you pay Rs 2,040 (mode 1).
  • Salary raise: from 800 to 1,000 is a 25% increase (mode 3).

The formulas

Percentage of: (X ÷ 100) × Y · Share: (X ÷ Y) × 100 · Change: ((new − old) ÷ old) × 100.

How do I calculate a discount price?

Use mode 1 to find the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price. Example: 20% of 1,500 is 300, so the sale price is 1,200.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

If a rate rises from 10% to 12%, that is +2 percentage points but a +20% relative increase. Mode 3 measures the relative increase.

QR Code Generator

Turn any link, text, phone number or Wi-Fi detail into a scannable QR code and download it as a PNG — free, no watermark, no expiry.

The code is generated on your device. QR codes made here are static — they never expire and work offline once printed.

How to use

  1. Paste a link or type any text (a phone number works too — use the format tel:+92… to make it dialable).
  2. Pick a size: 300px is fine on screen; choose 800px for printing.
  3. Download the PNG and share or print it.

Tips for codes that scan first time

  • Keep strong contrast: dark code on a light background.
  • Print at least 2 × 2 cm for arm's-length scanning.
  • Short links make simpler, easier-to-scan codes — shorten long URLs first.
  • Always test with your own phone before printing in bulk.
Do QR codes from this tool expire?

No. These are static QR codes — the information is stored in the pattern itself, with no redirect service in between, so they work forever.

Can I make a QR code for Wi-Fi?

Yes. Enter text in this format: WIFI:T:WPA;S:YourNetwork;P:YourPassword;; — most phone cameras will offer to join the network when scanned.

Password Generator

Create strong, random passwords on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server — the password exists only on this screen until you copy it.

What makes a password strong?

Length beats cleverness. A random 16-character password drawn from letters, digits and symbols has about 104 bits of entropy — far beyond brute-force reach. Every extra character multiplies the combinations an attacker must try.

Rules that actually keep accounts safe

  • Use a different password for every account — reuse is how one leak becomes ten.
  • Store passwords in a password manager, not in notes or chats.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication wherever it is offered — Fayaz's own accounts use it, and it blocks most account takeovers even if a password leaks.
  • Never share a generated password by screenshot; copy it straight into the sign-up form.
Can this site see or store my password?

No. Generation uses your browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues) and runs entirely on your device. The history list lives only in this tab's memory and disappears when you close the page.

How long should my password be?

At least 14–16 characters for important accounts like email and banking. This generator goes up to 64 for passphrases and API keys.

Loan / EMI Calculator

Monthly installment (EMI), total interest and total repayment for a home, car, bike or personal loan — with a year-by-year balance breakdown.

The EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly payments. This is the reducing-balance method banks use: interest is charged only on what you still owe, so the interest share shrinks every month.

Worked example

A loan of 1,000,000 at 12% for 5 years gives an EMI of about 22,244 per month, total interest of about 334,667 and a total repayment of about 1,334,667. Shortening the tenure raises the EMI but cuts total interest sharply — try 3 years above and compare.

Before you sign

  • Ask for processing fees, insurance and early-settlement charges — they are not in the EMI.
  • Check whether the rate is fixed or floating (KIBOR-linked in Pakistan).
  • Keep total EMIs below roughly 30–40% of monthly income.
Does this calculator match my bank's EMI?

It uses the same standard formula, so figures should match to the nearest rupee for a simple reducing-balance loan. Differences appear when a bank adds fees or uses a flat-rate product — always confirm with the bank's own schedule.

What is the difference between flat and reducing rate?

A flat rate charges interest on the full original amount for the whole tenure, which costs far more. A “10% flat” loan is roughly equal to a 19% reducing-balance loan over 5 years.

How to compress an image

  1. Choose or drop an image.
  2. Set quality — 70–80% is the sweet spot: much smaller files, no visible difference for web and sharing.
  3. Optionally cap the width (1280px suits blogs and listings).
  4. Download the compressed JPG.

Why size matters

A phone photo can be 4–8 MB. Compressed to ~300 KB it uploads 15× faster, uses less mobile data, and pages carrying it load quicker — page speed is a Google ranking factor and a direct reason visitors stay or leave. Output is always JPEG, which also converts PNG photos to a much smaller file (transparency is flattened onto white).

Will compression ruin my photo's quality?

At 75–85% quality, the difference is invisible on screens for normal photos. For printing or archiving, keep your original file as well — compression here is for sharing and web use.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed with your browser's canvas on your own device. You can verify this by loading the page, going offline, and compressing — it still works.

Step by step: Google Docs method

  1. Open Google Drive and upload the PDF.
  2. Right-click the file → Open with → Google Docs. Wait for conversion.
  3. Check headings, tables and page breaks — fix what shifted.
  4. File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx).

Scanned PDFs need OCR

If the PDF Inspector above extracts no text, your PDF is a scanned image. Use an OCR tool (Google Drive's conversion includes basic OCR; dedicated OCR sites handle Urdu and mixed-language scans better). Expect to proofread names and numbers — OCR confuses similar-looking characters.

Why doesn't this site convert PDF to Word directly?

Because an honest browser-only conversion isn't possible at acceptable quality: rebuilding Word paragraphs, tables and fonts from PDF drawing commands requires a heavy document engine. Rather than upload your private documents or hand you a broken file, we give you the working text extractor plus the methods that do the full job.

Is the PDF Inspector safe for private documents?

Yes — the PDF is read by JavaScript on your device only. It is never transmitted, so even confidential files stay confidential.

How do I go the other way — Word to PDF?

In Word or Google Docs use File → Download / Save As → PDF. That direction is lossless and built into both apps for free.

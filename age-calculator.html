Age Calculator

Your exact age in years, months and days — plus total days lived, the weekday you were born, and when your next birthday falls.

Why an exact age matters

Forms for exams, jobs, passports and school admissions often ask for age “as on” a specific date — for example, age on 31 December of the admission year. Set the second date to that cut-off to get the exact figure the form expects, including whether a birthday has passed yet.

How it works

The calculator subtracts the dates properly across month lengths and leap years, then breaks the span into years, months and days, and also totals it in days, weeks and hours.

How many days have I lived?

Enter your birth date and leave the second date as today — the “Total days” figure in the result is your exact count, leap days included.

What weekday was I born on?

It is shown in the result. The weekday is computed from the calendar date itself, so it is correct for any date the picker allows.
